    Import categories from Excel / Google Sheets

    Columns: Category, Unit (sq ft, sq in or pcs), Price, Minimum. For prices by quantity, put pcs as the unit and the price like this: 1-12:150; 13-24:100; 25+:80. Select the rows in Excel, copy, and paste them here. A category with the same name gets its price updated.

    Backup and import

    Your data is saved in this browser. Export now and then to keep a backup, or to move it to another computer.

    Import items from CSV / Google Sheets

    Paste columns Name, Category, Unit, Cost here (copied straight from Google Sheets or Excel). For packs, add a 5th column Pieces per pack; the Cost is then the price of the whole pack.
